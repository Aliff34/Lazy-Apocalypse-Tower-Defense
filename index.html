How to play

A practical first-run route, progression priorities and ways to play on a phone or PC.

YOUR FIRST RUN

From menu to first upgrades

  1. Start Main mode. Your chair stays in place; weapons fire automatically as zombies enter range.
  2. Collect XP likes. Move through the run and gather drops to level up. Spend each level on a temporary combat stat.
  3. Build around what you equip. Early on, Damage and Attack Speed are broadly useful. Add Max HP or Regen if you are getting overwhelmed; choose XP or coin gains when building economy.
  4. Watch enemy mix and bosses. Tanks take longer to clear; Speedy, Ranged and Kamikaze enemies can create sudden pressure. Use gadget skills when a dangerous group reaches you.
  5. Finish the run and collect rewards. Coins, parts, chests and followers feed your permanent account. A run ending still advances meta systems.
PROGRESSION ROUTE

What to focus on next

  1. Push your best wave record. New records open features, leagues, part tiers and rewards.
  2. Keep your loadout current. Merge spare matching parts, improve rarity when practical, and recycle extras you do not need.
  3. Claim quests and offline Radar. Check daily and weekly tasks, event tasks and stored Radar rewards between runs.
  4. Invest in permanent upgrades. Spend coins on Golden Upgrade; once unlocked, use CPU in the Attack or Survival Technology Tree.
  5. Try side modes after they unlock. Gold Rush helps coin income; Boss Rush rewards part chests. Save tickets for the mode you need.

PRACTICAL TIPS

Small decisions, longer runs

  • Choose damage and attack speed to shorten fights; layer in HP and regeneration if survival is the bottleneck.
  • Use XP and coin run bonuses early when they help fund more upgrades; take combat stats when the next wave is the immediate threat.
  • Equip gadgets for their utility: healing, pushback, crowd control, shield or turret support.
  • Use the one revive strategically when it can extend a strong run or protect a valuable record.
  • Compare choices against the enemies and boss patterns that are actually ending your runs.

UNDERSTAND RUN UPGRADES

What should I pick?

Damage: each hit becomes stronger. A dependable pick when zombies are taking too long to defeat.

Attack Speed: weapons fire more often. It helps builds that rely on frequent hits or status effects.

Max HP and Regen: increase your health buffer and recovery. Favor them when the run ends because you run out of health.

Critical chance and multiplier: improve how often critical hits happen and how hard they hit.

Range: lets weapons engage farther away; useful for buying time as waves advance.

XP and Coins: improve run income. Choose economy while the current wave is safe; favor combat stats if you are close to losing.

AFTER A RUN

Turn a loss into progress

  1. Review the result and collect coins, followers, chests and parts.
  2. Use Second Chance once if it can save a meaningful run; the notes describe it as an ad revive.
  3. Return to the Workshop: merge useful duplicate parts, equip the best available tier and recycle spare parts.
  4. Spend coins on Golden Upgrades, claim quest rewards and collect offline Radar income.
  5. Try another Main run to push your record. Enter Gold Rush or Boss Rush when you need coins or part chests and have a ticket or entry available.

The best next step depends on what stopped the run: damage, survivability or equipment strength.

Combat & upgrades

Stay seated. Let the hardware do the work. Make every XP pick count.

THE RUNMAIN MODE · ENDLESS

Automatic fire, manual decisions

Your character stays still while equipped weapons automatically attack. Some gadgets have cooldown skills you trigger manually or automate with Auto Skill. Waves continue endlessly; bosses appear at intervals.

  • Enemies drop XP (“likes”), coins and loot. Every wave adds a bonus.
  • Spend run XP on temporary stats; those levels reset after the run.
  • Second Chance can revive you once per run for an ad.
  • Ad speed-ups unlock from wave 3; SpeedyPass adds permanent speed.

SURVIVE / 03

Permanent progression

Runs end. Your account upgrades stick around.

TWO LAYERS OF POWER

Run upgrades reset

During Main mode, XP likes buy temporary stat levels. These help you survive the current attempt and disappear when the run ends.

Meta upgrades persist

Coins, parts, CPU, Coffee, pets and artifacts develop your account between runs. Use each system’s currency for its matching permanent upgrade.

Simple loop: push your wave record → collect run rewards → improve parts and permanent upgrades → try again with a stronger build.

CHESTS & REWARDS

Chest sources

Daily, Chapter, PvE post-run, XP, Battle Pass, Shop and leaderboard chests. The first PvE chest is guaranteed after two runs (per notes).

Shop & roulette

Super Box unlocks from wave 20. Mega Roulette has one free and five ad spins daily; a Mega Chest every six spins. Chest slots open on a timer and can be sped with gems or ads.
